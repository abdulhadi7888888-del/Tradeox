Risk Management — Position Size

Formula: Risk amount = Capital × Risk%. Uske baad Qty = Risk amount ÷ (Entry − Stop Loss ki distance). Matlab agar SL hit ho jaye, to sirf utna hi loss hoga jitna aapne "Risk %" mein set kiya — chahe coin kitna bhi volatile ho. Stop-loss discipline: SL lagne ke baad usay move mat karein, aur ek trade mein apne total capital ka risk % kabhi 1-2% se zyada na rakhein.
